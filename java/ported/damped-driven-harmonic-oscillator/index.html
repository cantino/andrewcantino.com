Exploring the Damped Driven Harmonic Oscillator

Click or drag on either graph to set the driving frequency ω.

A mass on a spring driven by an oscillating support. The top graph shows the steady-state phase lag between drive and response; the bottom graph shows the response amplitude. Both peak as the drive frequency ω approaches the natural frequency ωo — the resonance — and the sharpness of that peak is set by Q.
